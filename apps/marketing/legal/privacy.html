Privacy policy

How we handle your data.

Information we collect

When you request access or contact us, we collect: name, email, organization name, role, and the information you voluntarily provide. When you use the Atlas application, we collect: user identity, session data, and records of your actions (conflict resolutions, report generations, document uploads) for audit purposes.

How we use information

To provide and improve the Atlas service. To respond to access requests and support inquiries. To maintain an audit trail of investigation decisions. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Data retention

Session data is retained until expiry (8 hours default) and swept every 10 minutes. Audit logs, claims, conflicts, and reports are retained per your organization's retention policy. Lead/contact information is retained until you request deletion.

Data sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except as required to operate the service (infrastructure providers) or as required by law. Subprocessors are listed in our compliance documentation.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. You may export your data at any time via the Atlas API.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: legal@atlas-argus.com
