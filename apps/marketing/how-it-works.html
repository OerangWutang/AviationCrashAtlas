How it works

From source data to defensible report in five steps.

1. Ingest

Upload PDF dockets, connect NTSB eADMS data, or submit structured claims via API. Every submission is fingerprinted and stored as an immutable raw snapshot.

2. Reconcile

The system extracts field-level claims and detects conflicts when sources disagree. Open conflicts appear in the reviewer queue for resolution.

3. Resolve

Reviewers pick a winning claim or enter a manual override with required rationale. The decision is recorded in the activity log with optimistic concurrency protection.

4. Project

The projection is rebuilt from active claims and conflict decisions. Every fact shows its provenance, source reliability, and evidence chain.

5. Export

Generate immutable, version-controlled PDF reports. Every output fact includes a footnote tracing back to its ingestion timestamp and source.

Core philosophy

"The projected public record is not the truth. The evidence chain is the truth. The projection is the current best explanation of that evidence."
