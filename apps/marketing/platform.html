Platform

Claims-based evidence integrity for aviation safety data.

The evidence chain

The Atlas platform is built around a simple but rigorous idea: every projected fact in the system is strictly accountable to an immutable evidence chain.

Ingest
Source data from NTSB, FAA, FOQA, ASAP, maintenance logs, depositions
→
Extract
Field-level claims with source fingerprint and reliability tier
→
Reconcile
Conflict detection between disagreeing sources
→
Resolve
Reviewer selects winner with required rationale
→
Export
Immutable report with every fact footnoted to its source

Architecture

Claims model

Each field is stored as an independent claim with source provenance, ingestion timestamp, and reliability tier. The projection is a derived view — not the truth itself.

Conflict resolution

When sources disagree, the system surfaces an explicit conflict rather than silently resolving. Resolutions require a rationale and are recorded in an immutable activity log.

Provenance

Every fact links back to its source claim, ingestion run, and raw snapshot. Audit hashes enable independent verification of data integrity.

Tenant isolation

Enterprise tenants operate in isolated PostgreSQL schemas with row-level security. Private evidence never leaks into public views.
