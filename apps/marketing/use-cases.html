Use cases

Who Atlas serves and how.

Aviation litigation

Plaintiff and defense attorneys need to reconcile conflicting evidence across NTSB reports, FAA records, maintenance logs, and depositions. Atlas surfaces every disagreement and produces court-submissible reports with source-footnoted facts.

Safety investigation

NTSB, TSBC, and international investigation authorities manage complex evidence sets with multiple contributing sources. Atlas provides a structured evidence chain that preserves the integrity of every factual assertion.

Airline safety teams

Internal safety departments track FOQA events, ASAP reports, and maintenance findings. Atlas enables structured cross-referencing with public accident data for trend analysis and risk assessment.

Insurance & risk

Aviation insurers and risk assessors need reliable fact bases for underwriting and claims analysis. Atlas provides traceable evidence chains that withstand scrutiny.
