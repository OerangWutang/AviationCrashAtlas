About

Building the defensible record for aviation safety.

Why Atlas exists

Aviation accident investigations produce an immense volume of data from multiple sources: NTSB factual reports, FAA records, maintenance logs, witness statements, FOQA/ASAP data, news coverage, and depositions. These sources often disagree on specific facts — a tail number recorded one way in one database and another in a different database, or a timeline that contradicts itself across reports.

Atlas was built to solve this: instead of silently picking a "best guess," it makes every disagreement visible, trackable, and resolvable with a clear rationale. Every projected fact is accountable to an evidence chain.

Domain expertise

The Atlas team combines software engineering, aviation safety investigation, and legal domain knowledge. The system is designed for use by NTSB-trained investigators, airline safety teams, and aviation litigation attorneys who demand rigorous evidence traceability.

Open source

Atlas Argus (the core backend) is open-source software. The frontend and BFF follow the same architecture. You can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host. Enterprise features (tenant isolation, SSO, audit reporting) are available under license.
